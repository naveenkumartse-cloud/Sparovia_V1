## 1. Vision Statement

Sparovia aims to become a **multi-tenant business experience and intelligence platform** that helps businesses establish their digital presence, understand their customers, manage their business interactions, and continuously identify opportunities for improvement.

Sparovia should move beyond being a traditional website platform or Admin Panel.

The long-term vision is:

```text
Understand the Business
        ↓
Establish Digital Presence
        ↓
Manage Customer Interactions
        ↓
Understand Business Activity
        ↓
Identify Opportunities
        ↓
Recommend Actions
        ↓
Assist With Execution
        ↓
Measure Outcomes
        ↓
Improve Continuously
