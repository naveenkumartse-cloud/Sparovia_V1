> **Status: LOCKED**
>
> This document defines the approved MVP scope for Sparovia.
> Future capabilities may be architecturally planned but must not be implemented unless they belong to the approved phase.

---

# 1. Product Overview

Sparovia is a multi-tenant business experience and intelligence platform.

The MVP focuses on creating a strong foundation around:

- Business onboarding
- Business profile
- Premium website experience
- Basic Admin Panel
- Content and media management
- Lead management
- Digital presence foundation
- AI-ready architecture
- Secure multi-tenant infrastructure

The MVP must remain intentionally focused.

The goal is not to build the complete long-term Sparovia vision immediately.

---
