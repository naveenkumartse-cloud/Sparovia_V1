# Sparovia — Product-Wide UI/UX Modernization Program
## Master Task Specification

### Objective
Transform the existing Sparovia application into a premium, modern, professional, and effortless Business Experience Platform with consistent Editorial Cobalt & Restrained Purple visual design, mobile-first responsiveness (320px to 1440px), client-first workflow simplicity, and rigorous accessibility and stability.

### Product Authority & Bound Constraints
- Document Authority: `docs/RULES.md`, `docs/SPAROVIA_MASTER_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md`, `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `.agents/skills/sparovia-ui-ux-standard/SKILL.md`.
- Brand Tokens: Editorial Cobalt `#315FEA`, Restrained Purple `#7950B8`, WhatsApp `#25D366` (channel-only).
- Shape Language: 4px indicator, 6px control, 8px panel, 10px dialog. No pill-shaped buttons.
- Functional Protection: No backend rebuild, no customer-facing Theme System, preserve image lifecycle and immutability, preserve leads contracts, preserve WhatsApp templates and social profiles without paid Meta API or automation, preserve draft/publish separation.

### Batches
1. Batch 0: Complete Application Experience Research & Screen Inventory
2. Batch 1: Design Foundation, Tokens, Glass/Purple Primitives & Mobile-First Application Shell
3. Batch 2: Core Business Workflows (Command Center, Business Context, Business Presence, Content Management, Onboarding)
4. Batch 3: AI & Media Experiences (AI Content Assistance, Image Studio visual alignment, Website Images & Explore Our Work)
5. Batch 4: Daily Operations & Communication (Leads, WhatsApp Templates, Social Links, AI Connections, Account & Settings)
6. Batch 5: Cross-Application Consistency, Accessibility, Motion Safety, and Full Verification
